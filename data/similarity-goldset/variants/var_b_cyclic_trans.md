# Procedure of Environmental Qualification Test for Aircraft Equipment

<!-- Page 1 -->

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the methodology of environmental qualification test which
is applicable to airborne equipment intended for the platforms of fixed wing and
rotary wing operating inside the envelope of defense aviation. The procedures
herein establish the baseline of qualification of minimum that the suppliers
must demonstrate before the acceptance of delivery. The deviation from this
baseline requires written approval from the engineering authority that is
responsible.

## 2. Tailoring of Program

The matrix of applicability inside Table 2-1 specifies which sequences of test
must be performed for each category of equipment. The decisions of tailoring
shall consider the operational profile, the criticality of mission, and the
heritage of prior qualification of the candidate equipment. The supplier shall
submit a memorandum of rationale of tailoring to the procuring authority within
14 days from the contract award. The reuse of legacy data of qualification is
permitted when the profile of platform exposure is bounded by the prior test.

## 3. Cited Standards

The publications which follow are invoked to the extent that is indicated inside
the sections that follow. Where conflicts arise between this document and any
reference that is cited, this document shall govern. MIL-STD-810H establishes
the methods of laboratory test. RTCA DO-160G provides the coverage of commercial
transport. SAE AS6171 addresses the avoidance of counterfeit during the
preparation of test sample. ISO 16750 covers the interfaces of ground vehicle.

## 4. The Environment of Laboratory

### 4.1 The Climatic Chamber

The testing of temperature, humidity, and altitude shall be conducted inside
a chamber capable of maintaining setpoints inside plus or minus 2 degrees
Celsius and 5 percent of relative humidity. The volume of work of the chamber
shall provide at the minimum 150 millimeters of clearance around the article
of test on all the sides. The time of recovery of chamber after the opening
of door shall not exceed 10 minutes for the setpoints which are nominal.

### 4.2 The Vibration Apparatus

The mechanical vibration shall be applied using one shaker which is
electrodynamic capable of producing the spectra defined inside Section 7. The
fixture shall provide one ratio of transmissibility which is measured between
0.9 and 1.1 across the bandwidth of test. The control of acceleration shall
be performed using accelerometers which are triaxial bonded directly to the
interface of mounting of the article of test.

## 5. Cycling of Temperature

### 5.1 Definition of Profile

The thermal profile is composed of 12 cycles which are successive between minus
40 and plus 71 degrees Celsius with one time of dwell of 60 minutes at each
extreme. The rates of transition shall not exceed 5 degrees Celsius per minute
unless the design of equipment explicitly accommodates the excursions which
are faster. The power shall be applied during the two final cycles in order to
verify the performance which is functional under the stress which is thermal.

### 5.2 Pass Criteria

The equipment shall remain inside the limits of specification during the
cycles which are powered and shall recover the functionality which is full
inside 5 minutes from returning to the conditions which are ambient. The visible
damage including cracking, warping, or the degradation of seal constitutes a
failure of test independent of the performance which is functional. The supplier
shall document any indications which are anomalous which are observed during
the sequence of cycling.

## 6. The Exposure to Humidity

The test of humidity is composed of 10 cycles which are consecutive of 24 hours
inside which the relative humidity of the chamber is maintained above 95 percent
with the temperatures cycling between 30 and 60 degrees Celsius. The condensation
on the exterior of the equipment is permitted but the intrusion of moisture
internal shall result in the failure of test. The checks which are functional
shall be performed at the conclusion of each cycle and at the end of the
sequence of exposure.

## 7. Random Vibration

### 7.1 Selection of Spectrum

The spectrum of random vibration shall be selected from the categories defined
inside Table 7-1 based on the zone of installation of platform. The installations
of helicopter require the spectrum of rotorcraft with the content of low
frequency which is elevated. The installations of fuselage of fixed wing shall
use the spectrum of jet aircraft unless the proximity of engine warrants the
spectrum of propeller.

### 7.2 The Duration

Each axis shall be subjected to the vibration during 60 minutes per axis for
one exposure which is total of 180 minutes. The spectrum shall be applied
sequentially along the three axes which are orthogonal corresponding to the
orientation of installation of equipment. The monitoring of performance during
the 5 minutes which are final of each axis shall confirm the absence of
amplification which is resonant.

## 8. The Testing of Shock

The pulses of shock which are operational of 20 g of amplitude with 11
milliseconds of duration shall be applied along each of the six principal axes.
The shape of pulse shall approximate a waveform which is half sine inside plus
or minus 15 percent. The pulses of shock of safety of crash of 75 g shall be
applied in the direction which is forward only and need not be powered during
the application.

## 9. Documentation

A consolidated report of qualification shall summarize all the results of test,
the anomalies, and the actions which are corrective in accordance with the list
of requirements of contract data. The report shall be retained during the lifetime
of operation of the equipment which is qualified and made available to the
procuring authority upon request.
