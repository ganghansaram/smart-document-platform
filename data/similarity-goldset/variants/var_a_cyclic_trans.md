# Procedure for EMC Testing of Avionics Equipment

<!-- Page 1 -->

## 1. Range of Application

This standard sets up the test procedure and requirements for measuring the
characteristics of electromagnetic compatibility for avionics equipment to be
mounted on the platforms of military aircraft. The procedure indicated here
applies to all equipment that processes, transmits, or receives electrical
signals during normal operation. The compliance with this standard is mandatory
unless it is specified differently by the agency of procurement.

## 2. Application Scope

The requirements of this standard shall apply to all line replaceable units (LRU)
and shop replaceable units (SRU) operating in the radio frequency range of 30 Hz
to 18 GHz. The equipment that is exempt from these requirements includes
mechanical assemblies which are pure and devices powered by battery whose
operating duration is below 60 seconds. The contractor shall identify all the
equipment of candidate according to the system specification that is applicable.

## 3. Reference Documents

The documents that follow constitute a part of this specification within the
limit specified here. In the case where there is conflict between the text of
this specification and the references that are cited, the text of this
specification shall take the precedence. MIL-STD-461G governs the limits of
emission and susceptibility. MIL-STD-464D defines requirements at the level of
system. RTCA DO-160G provides the guidance for commercial equivalence.

## 4. General Conditions of Test

### 4.1 The Surrounding Environment

All the tests shall be performed inside an enclosure that is shielded providing
attenuation of minimum 100 dB between 10 kHz and 10 GHz. The temperature of
ambient inside the enclosure shall be maintained between 18 degrees Celsius and
28 degrees Celsius. The relative humidity shall not exceed 75 percent during
any sequence of test. The environment of ambient electromagnetic shall be at
the minimum 6 dB below the emission limit which is the lowest specified.

### 4.2 The Configuration of Equipment

The equipment under test shall be configured in the mode of normal operation
with all the cables of interconnection installed as defined inside the drawing
of installation. The cables of input power shall be routed through one network
of stabilization for line impedance (LISN) which has impedance of 50
microhenries in parallel with 50 ohms. The equipment shall be bonded to the
plane of ground using the methods that are specified inside the document of
installation control.

## 5. Conducted Emissions

### 5.1 Setup of Test

The conducted emissions on the leads of power shall be measured by means of
a current probe positioned at 5 centimeters from the connector of the equipment.
The measurement receiver shall use a bandwidth of resolution of 1 kHz from
30 Hz to 1 kHz, a bandwidth of 10 kHz from 1 kHz to 100 kHz, and a bandwidth
of 100 kHz over 100 kHz. The mode of peak detection shall be used for all the
measurements unless otherwise specifically directed.

### 5.2 The Limits

The narrowband emissions shall not exceed the levels defined inside Figure 5-1
for the equipment of Class A or Figure 5-2 for the equipment of Class B. The
broadband emissions shall comply with the limits inside Figure 5-3. The discrete
emissions exceeding the limit by greater than 3 dB shall be reported individually
with the frequency, amplitude, and bandwidth that are recorded inside the report
of test.

## 6. Radiated Emissions

### 6.1 Configuration of Antenna

A biconical antenna shall be used from 30 MHz to 200 MHz, a log periodic antenna
from 200 MHz to 1 GHz, and a horn antenna over 1 GHz. The antenna shall be
positioned at 1 meter from the front face of the equipment under test. The
measurements of vertical and horizontal polarization shall be performed at each
frequency.

### 6.2 The Limits

The radiated emissions shall not exceed the limits specified inside Table 6-1.
Where the equipment exhibits emissions inside 3 dB of the limit, additional
measurements shall be performed in order to verify the result. The contractor
shall provide the rationale for any request of exceedance which is submitted to
the agency of procurement.

## 7. Requirements of Susceptibility

The equipment under test shall not exhibit malfunction, degradation of the
performance, or deviation from the specified parameters when subjected to the
levels of radiated and conducted susceptibility of Table 7-1. The criteria of
performance for each mode of operation shall be defined inside the specification
of equipment. The recovery without intervention of operator shall be required
after the removal of the stimulus of susceptibility.

## 8. Test Report

A formal report of test shall be prepared in accordance with the list of
requirements of contract data (CDRL). The report shall include the photographs
of test setup, the data of calibration for all the equipment of measurement,
the complete plots of the data which is measured, and a tabulation of all the
exceedances with the corrective action that is proposed. The report shall be
submitted within 30 calendar days from the completion of test.
