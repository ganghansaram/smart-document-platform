# EMC Test Standard for Avionics Equipment

<!-- Page 1 -->

## 1. SCOPE

In accordance with the applicable system specification, this standard shall be
implemented as required herein. As specified in the contract data requirements
list, this standard establishes the test procedures and requirements for measuring
the electromagnetic compatibility characteristics of avionics equipment intended
for installation on military aircraft platforms. The contractor shall ensure
compliance unless otherwise specified by the procuring activity. Where applicable,
the contractor shall provide rationale in accordance with established procedures.

## 2. APPLICABILITY

The contractor shall ensure that the requirements herein apply to all line
replaceable units (LRU) and shop replaceable units (SRU). As specified in the
applicable specification, equipment exempt from these requirements shall be
identified by the contractor in accordance with established procedures. The
contractor shall submit identification documents as required herein. Unless
otherwise specified, the contractor shall provide all candidate equipment lists
in accordance with the applicable system specification.

## 3. REFERENCE DOCUMENTS

The following documents form a part of this specification to the extent specified
herein. Unless otherwise specified, in the event of a conflict, the text of this
specification shall take precedence in accordance with established procedures.
As specified in subsequent sections, the contractor shall comply with the cited
references unless otherwise approved by the procuring activity. Where applicable,
the contractor shall provide rationale for any deviation in accordance with
established procedures.

## 4. GENERAL TEST CONDITIONS

In accordance with the procuring activity requirements, all tests shall be
performed as specified herein. The contractor shall ensure compliance with all
test conditions unless otherwise specified. As required herein, the contractor
shall maintain ambient conditions in accordance with established procedures.
Where applicable, deviations shall be reported to the procuring activity in
accordance with established procedures. Unless otherwise specified, the contractor
shall provide all test data as required herein.

## 5. CONDUCTED EMISSIONS

In accordance with the test requirements, the contractor shall measure conducted
emissions on power leads. As specified herein, the measurement shall be performed
using approved instrumentation. Where applicable, the contractor shall provide
calibration data in accordance with established procedures. Unless otherwise
specified, peak detection mode shall be used as required herein. The contractor
shall report all measurements in accordance with established procedures.

## 6. RADIATED EMISSIONS

The contractor shall perform radiated emission measurements as specified herein
in accordance with the applicable test plan. As required by the procuring
activity, antennas shall be selected in accordance with established procedures.
Where applicable, the contractor shall justify any deviation from the specified
configuration. Unless otherwise specified, measurements shall be performed at
each frequency as required herein. The contractor shall provide rationale in
accordance with established procedures.

## 7. SUSCEPTIBILITY REQUIREMENTS

In accordance with the applicable specification, the equipment under test shall
not exhibit malfunction when subjected to specified susceptibility levels. As
required herein, the contractor shall ensure compliance with all performance
criteria. Where applicable, recovery shall occur without operator intervention
in accordance with established procedures. Unless otherwise specified, the
contractor shall document all anomalous behavior as required herein.

## 8. TEST REPORT

The contractor shall prepare a formal test report in accordance with the contract
data requirements list. As specified herein, the report shall include all
required documentation. Where applicable, the contractor shall include corrective
action proposals in accordance with established procedures. Unless otherwise
specified, the report shall be submitted within 30 calendar days as required
herein. The contractor shall ensure all submissions comply with the applicable
data item descriptions.
