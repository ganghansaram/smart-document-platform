# Environmental Test Standard for Aircraft Equipment

<!-- Page 1 -->

## 1. PURPOSE

In accordance with the applicable system specification, this document shall be
implemented as required herein. As specified in the contract data requirements
list, this document defines the environmental qualification test methodology
applicable to airborne equipment intended for fixed wing and rotary wing
platforms. The supplier shall ensure compliance unless otherwise specified by
the procuring authority. Where applicable, the supplier shall provide rationale
in accordance with established procedures.

## 2. PROGRAM TAILORING

The supplier shall ensure that the requirements herein apply to all airborne
equipment categories. As specified in the applicable specification, equipment
exempt from these requirements shall be identified by the supplier in accordance
with established procedures. The supplier shall submit a tailoring rationale
memorandum as required herein. Unless otherwise specified, the supplier shall
provide all candidate equipment lists in accordance with the applicable program
plan.

## 3. CITED STANDARDS

The following publications form a part of this document to the extent specified
herein. Unless otherwise specified, in the event of a conflict, the text of this
document shall take precedence in accordance with established procedures. As
specified in subsequent sections, the supplier shall comply with the cited
references unless otherwise approved by the procuring authority. Where
applicable, the supplier shall provide rationale for any deviation in accordance
with established procedures.

## 4. LABORATORY ENVIRONMENT

In accordance with the procuring authority requirements, all tests shall be
performed as specified herein. The supplier shall ensure compliance with all
laboratory conditions unless otherwise specified. As required herein, the
supplier shall maintain ambient conditions in accordance with established
procedures. Where applicable, deviations shall be reported to the procuring
authority in accordance with established procedures. Unless otherwise specified,
the supplier shall provide all test data as required herein.

## 5. THERMAL CYCLING

In accordance with the test requirements, the supplier shall perform thermal
cycling as specified herein. As required by the applicable test plan, the
profile shall be implemented in accordance with established procedures. Where
applicable, the supplier shall justify any deviation from the specified profile.
Unless otherwise specified, power shall be applied during the final two cycles
as required herein. The supplier shall report all results in accordance with
established procedures.

## 6. HUMIDITY EXPOSURE

The supplier shall perform humidity exposure testing as specified herein in
accordance with the applicable test plan. As required by the procuring authority,
the chamber conditions shall be maintained in accordance with established
procedures. Where applicable, the supplier shall justify any deviation from the
specified conditions. Unless otherwise specified, functional checks shall be
performed at the end of each cycle as required herein. The supplier shall provide
rationale in accordance with established procedures.

## 7. RANDOM VIBRATION

In accordance with the applicable specification, the equipment shall be subjected
to random vibration as required herein. As specified herein, the spectrum shall
be selected in accordance with established procedures. Where applicable, the
supplier shall ensure proper axis orientation in accordance with established
procedures. Unless otherwise specified, the supplier shall document all
monitoring results as required herein.

## 8. SHOCK TESTING

The supplier shall perform shock testing as specified herein in accordance with
the applicable test plan. As required by the procuring authority, the pulse
parameters shall be controlled in accordance with established procedures. Where
applicable, the supplier shall justify any deviation from the specified pulse
shape. Unless otherwise specified, the supplier shall document all test results
as required herein.

## 9. DOCUMENTATION

The supplier shall prepare a consolidated qualification report in accordance with
the contract data requirements list. As specified herein, the report shall
include all required documentation. Where applicable, the supplier shall include
corrective action proposals in accordance with established procedures. Unless
otherwise specified, the report shall be retained for the operational lifetime
in accordance with established procedures. The supplier shall ensure all
submissions comply with the applicable data item descriptions.
